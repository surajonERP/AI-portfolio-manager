How it works

  1. Tell us about yourself

    Your age, investment amount, monthly SIP, time horizon, target return, and how you react when markets fall.

  2. Get your allocation

    Your risk ability and willingness are scored separately, and the lower one decides your profile. It maps to a mix of Indian equity, gold, silver, fixed income and cash, with the option to hold equity through Nifty 100 stocks screened for your risk level.

  3. Explore and adjust

    See returns, risk, the efficient frontier and future projections for your portfolio. Move the weights yourself and watch every number update.
